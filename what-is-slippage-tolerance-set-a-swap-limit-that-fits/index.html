What Is Slippage Tolerance? Set a Swap Limit That Fits

Slippage tolerance in crypto swaps is the largest drop from the quoted output you’ll accept before the trade cancels; it sets your minimum received.

The Chain Times DeskNov 25, 2026 · 12:20 UTC2 min read

Slippage tolerance is the largest drop from a swap’s quoted output you’ll accept before the trade cancels. Pool prices can move between the quote and execution, so your tolerance sets the minimum output that will still let the swap go through.

Slippage vs price impact

Slippage is the gap between the output shown in a quote and the output you receive when the swap executes. It can happen when other trades reach the pool first, or when your own trade size moves the pool price.

Price impact is the price movement caused by your trade itself. In a constant-product pool, the token reserves follow x·y=k, so a larger trade changes the ratio between them and gets a less favorable price. Uniswap Docs explain how price impact affects a swap’s output and how a minimum-output setting can make the swap revert if that floor is missed.

Say you’re swapping TRC-20 tokens and the quote shows an output of 100 USDT. At 0.5% slippage tolerance, your minimum is 99.5 USDT: 100 × (1 − 0.005). If the swap would deliver less, it reverts instead of completing below your limit.

What slippage to set for stablecoin swaps

Stablecoin swaps usually suit a low tolerance because their pool prices tend to stay close. Thin pools or volatile token pairs may need more room for price movement, especially when your trade is large relative to the pool.

Too tight a setting can make swaps fail when the price moves past your minimum, while still costing a network fee. Too loose a setting lets a worse fill through. A loose tolerance also gives a sandwich bot more room: it can trade around your swap and push your execution toward the limit you accepted. Ethereum.org describes sandwich attacks as a form of MEV, or value extracted by arranging transactions around another trade. A wider setting raises the worst-case loss you allow.

On SunSwap, a TRON DEX for swapping TRC-20 tokens through liquidity pools, you can use the displayed minimum to judge the trade before approving it in TronLink. The wallet you use for a swap also needs a recovery plan; this publication looks at choosing paper or metal seed phrase backups. Pick a tolerance that leaves a minimum you would accept if the pool moves before execution.
