deBridge and Across quote differences: how routes price ETH

Why do deBridge and Across quotes show different amounts? Each route prices its own fees, gas and liquidity; compare estimated received amounts with minimums.

The Chain Times DeskNov 28, 2026 · 08:13 UTC3 min read

deBridge and Across quotes show different amounts because each route prices its own liquidity, execution and fees. For the same ETH transfer from Ethereum to Monad, compare the same source amount, destination token and recipient, then look at the estimated amount received and the minimum. Those figures help you judge what each route is offering.

For a Monad bridge transfer, keep those details the same before comparing options. Our comparison of which Monad bridge route costs less focuses on how to weigh the price of a transfer.

Why do deBridge and Across quotes differ?

deBridge and Across can source quotes through different providers and routes, so their costs and available liquidity can differ. Each quote reflects its route’s bridge or provider charge, gas on Ethereum and Monad, and the liquidity available for the transfer.

Across uses relayers, services that advance funds on the destination chain before settlement on the source chain. deBridge and Across can therefore build quotes from different routes and providers, each with its own costs and available funds.

Say you want to send ETH from Ethereum to Monad. Keep the source amount, destination token and recipient the same in both quotes. If one route has a lower provider charge while the other details match, the first can estimate more ETH received. Change only the available liquidity instead, and the route with more liquidity can offer a better estimate. These are illustrative cases: the quote reflects the route conditions at the time, not a fixed price.

Slippage means the output can shift as market prices change between the quote and execution, especially when a route swaps assets. Gas costs and route availability can also change. So a difference between two estimates can come from more than the headline provider charge.

Compare each quote in this order:

  1. Match the source amount, destination network and token, and recipient.
  2. Compare the estimated amount received, then the minimum received.
  3. Check when the quote expires, since the displayed amount may change when the quote updates.

Related questions

What does minimum received mean on a bridge quote?

On a Monad bridge quote, the minimum received is the quote’s execution floor: the least the route says you should receive under its quoted conditions. The estimate is a forecast; the minimum gives you a lower figure to use when comparing routes. A higher estimate can look better, but compare the minimum too before choosing.

Can a bridge quote change before I confirm?

Yes. deBridge and Across quotes can change as route availability, gas, liquidity and market conditions shift. Check the displayed amount and expiry again before confirming, and compare the updated estimate and minimum against the other route.
