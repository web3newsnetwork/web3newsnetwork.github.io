About Chain Times

Chain Times is an independent news site covering the crypto industry: markets, exchanges, DeFi, stablecoins, regulation, security and the networks themselves. We report on many projects and are not tied to any token, company or foundation.

We work from primary sources such as filings, on-chain data, official statements and code, and we say where each claim comes from. When we get something wrong, we fix it and note the change on the story. We are independent, and nothing we publish is financial advice.
