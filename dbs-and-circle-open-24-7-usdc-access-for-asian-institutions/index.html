DBS and Circle open 24/7 USDC access for Asian institutions

DBS and Circle say eligible institutions in Asia can mint and redeem USDC through the bank at any hour, adding a direct route beyond secondary markets.

The Chain Times DeskNov 21, 2026 · 06:52 UTC2 min read

DBS and Circle say eligible institutional clients in Asia will be able to mint and redeem USDC through the bank, with settlement available around the clock. In its announcement on Nov. 18, DBS said the service will let clients move between U.S. dollars and USDC at any time. USDC is a stablecoin, a digital token designed to track a currency’s value.

The arrangement gives institutions a way to get or redeem USDC through DBS instead of depending entirely on exchange markets. CoinDesk’s report said the bank route could offer corporate clients an alternative to crypto exchanges and other secondary markets.

How the DBS channel will work

Eligible institutional clients can mint USDC through DBS or redeem it through the bank. Minting means creating new tokens; redemption means exchanging tokens back for dollars. DBS and Circle said settlement between U.S. dollars and USDC will be near-instant and available 24 hours a day, seven days a week.

The firms said direct access could help clients meet treasury, settlement and liquidity needs, and reduce their reliance on secondary-market liquidity. They did not publish details on which institutions qualify, fees, transaction limits or when the service will become available.

Circle issues USDC; DBS provides client access

Circle issues USDC through its regulated affiliates, according to DBS. The announcement describes DBS as the bank through which eligible clients can mint and redeem the token. It does not say that DBS will issue USDC.

The partnership combines Circle’s stablecoin infrastructure with DBS’s banking and liquidity services, the companies said. Their announcement did not include figures for expected client use or transaction volumes.

The firms are also part of Project BLOOM

The USDC partnership builds on DBS and Circle’s participation in Project BLOOM, an initiative led by the Monetary Authority of Singapore. DBS said the project works on the use of tokenised money and interoperability between different forms of tokenised money and financial networks.

The new arrangement gives eligible institutions another way to access USDC through a bank. The announcement does not say how many clients will use the service or how much USDC they may mint or redeem.
