Visa adds Monad as a stablecoin settlement option

Visa has added Monad as a stablecoin settlement option for participating issuers and acquirers, expanding where they can settle payment obligations onchain.

The Chain Times DeskNov 11, 2026 · 15:48 UTC2 min read

Visa has added Monad as an option for participating card issuers and acquirers to settle obligations in stablecoins, giving them another blockchain network to use. The Monad Foundation’s announcement on Nov. 10 says the option is available now. A stablecoin is a crypto token designed to track the value of a currency such as the US dollar.

Visa’s Dan Roesbery, vice president of global onchain partnerships and go-to-market, said the addition gives partners more choice about which blockchain they use for settlement. The announcement does not name any issuers or acquirers using Monad, or say which stablecoins they can settle there.

What changes for Visa’s partners?

Participating issuers and acquirers can now use Monad to settle with Visa over a blockchain. Issuers are typically banks that provide cards; acquirers are banks or payment firms that process card payments for merchants. The Monad Foundation says this can let funds move over weekends and holidays, while traditional card settlement runs on a five-business-day banking schedule.

Monad is compatible with the software environment used by Ethereum. The foundation says partners that already use Ethereum-based custody, wallet and compliance tools can typically extend those integrations to Monad instead of building new ones. The Block also reported that Visa’s partners can use Monad for stablecoin settlement, but neither report identifies firms that have done so.

How much stablecoin settlement does Visa handle?

The Monad Foundation’s release says Visa reported a $25 billion annualized stablecoin settlement run rate as of September 2026, alongside more than 225 stablecoin-linked card programs live worldwide. An annualized run rate estimates a year’s activity from a shorter period. The figure covers Visa’s program overall; the announcement gives no settlement volume for Monad.

When will Monad’s privacy feature be ready?

The foundation says its Private Settlement feature is planned for the coming months. It is designed to let institutions keep sensitive settlement details confidential while sharing them with auditors or regulators when needed. The feature is not described as live today, and Visa’s announcement does not give a launch date.

For now, the confirmed change is an additional network option for Visa partners. The Block’s report on the addition likewise says the privacy feature is still in development.
