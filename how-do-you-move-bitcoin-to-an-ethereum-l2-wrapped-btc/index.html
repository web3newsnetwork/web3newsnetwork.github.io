How do you move Bitcoin to an Ethereum L2? Wrapped BTC

To move Bitcoin to an Ethereum layer 2, use a BTC-backed token: withdraw it to the network or bridge it from Ethereum, then check the balance in your L2 wallet.

The Chain Times DeskNov 23, 2026 · 16:52 UTC3 min read

To move Bitcoin to an Ethereum layer 2, get a BTC-backed token such as WBTC, cbBTC or tBTC onto Arbitrum or Optimism. These rollups settle to Ethereum and run Ethereum-style contracts, so native Bitcoin cannot live on them. RenBridge is an earlier route that helps put today’s options in context; see renbridge.net for the name.

Choose a route for your Bitcoin

Bitcoin reaches an L2 as a wrapped token, backed by BTC held elsewhere, through one of two routes.

  1. Start with your BTC. It can be on an exchange or in a Bitcoin wallet. Have the address for your L2 wallet ready, plus a small ETH balance on that network to pay gas, the fee for transactions.
  2. Choose a token the L2 accepts. WBTC, cbBTC and tBTC are BTC-backed ERC-20 tokens, a format used by Ethereum-style contracts. Check which of these your exchange or the L2 apps you plan to use accept. The token you receive needs to be supported on the destination network.
  3. Use the shorter route if your exchange offers it. Withdraw the chosen token directly to your L2 wallet, selecting the matching L2 network. Follow the exchange’s network and address prompts carefully: a token sent on the wrong network may not arrive where you expect.
  4. Otherwise, use Ethereum as the middle step. Wrap BTC on Ethereum to get a supported token, then deposit it through the L2’s canonical bridge, the network’s own route between Ethereum and the rollup. This route has an extra move and needs ETH for gas on Ethereum as well as on the L2.

Know what the return trip involves

Withdrawing from an optimistic rollup’s canonical bridge back to Ethereum can take about seven days because of its challenge period, the time allowed to contest a transaction. That makes the return trip much slower than the deposit. Plan for that delay before you bridge tokens onto Arbitrum or Optimism, especially if you expect to need them on Ethereum soon.

RenBridge once offered another route: it minted renBTC from native BTC. That original service was discontinued, so it is not today’s route for getting BTC onto an L2. Current options include WBTC, cbBTC and tBTC, depending on what the exchange or apps on your chosen network accept.

  1. Confirm the result. Open your wallet on the destination L2 and check that the wrapped BTC balance appears. If it does not show, add the token’s contract to the wallet’s display; the balance should then be visible on the L2. You are done when the correct token balance appears there.

Choose the BTC-backed token your destination network supports, then use the exchange withdrawal when available or the Ethereum bridge route when it is not.
