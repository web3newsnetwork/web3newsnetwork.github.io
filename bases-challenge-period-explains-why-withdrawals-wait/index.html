Base’s Challenge Period Explains Why Withdrawals Wait

A challenge period in optimistic rollups gives anyone seven days to dispute a Base state claim; withdrawals finalize on Ethereum only if the claim stands.

The Chain Times DeskNov 27, 2026 · 23:19 UTC3 min read

A Base withdrawal can finalize on Ethereum only after its output root has gone seven days without a successful dispute. That wait gives challengers time to contest a claim about Base’s state.

A Base withdrawal waits for its output root to clear review

In this illustrative case, you withdraw 1 ETH from Base. The withdrawal follows three stages: you initiate it on Base, prove it against a posted output root, then finalize it on Ethereum after the challenge window passes.

An output root is a claim about Base’s state at a given point. Under the OP Stack, a proposer posts that claim to Ethereum optimistically: it is accepted unless someone disputes it. Ethereum does not re-execute Base’s blocks to check every claim upfront, so the dispute window gives an honest challenger time to respond.

Before finalization, the withdrawal is proven but still waiting. After seven days without a successful challenge, it can be finalized on Ethereum. The amount and destination stay the same; what changes is that the withdrawal is now cleared to complete on Ethereum.

Base’s documented mainnet withdrawal path uses a seven-day finalization period, and Base runs permissionless fault proofs: anyone can challenge a claim. The Base bridge is the route in this example. For background on the route choice, how Base bridge and Across handle ETH withdrawals adds a comparison with Across Protocol.

A fault dispute game narrows the claim to one instruction

A FaultDisputeGame is the OP Stack process for resolving a challenged claim. The proposer and challenger take turns bisecting their claims, repeatedly narrowing the disputed part of execution until they reach one instruction. Ethereum then executes that instruction to decide which side was right.

Each side has a 3.5-day chess clock. A move uses time from the moving side’s clock; the other side’s clock applies when it is their turn. Since the two clocks total seven days, the game cannot run beyond that total even if it takes many moves. This bounds the dispute period while leaving time for each side to answer.

Both sides post bonds. The losing side’s bond pays the winner, making false claims and spam challenges costly. The seven-day window also gives an honest challenger time to get a transaction included on Ethereum, even during censorship or outages.

Related

Why does withdrawing from Base take 7 days?

Withdrawing from Base takes seven days because the output root used to prove the withdrawal must remain undisputed through the challenge window. A successful dispute can change which claim is accepted.

Do deposits to Base have a challenge period?

Deposits to Base do not use this withdrawal challenge period. The seven-day wait described here applies to withdrawals proved against an output root on Ethereum.
