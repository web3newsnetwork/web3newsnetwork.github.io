Editorial standards

What we cover

Chain Times reports on Crypto: markets, exchanges, protocols, regulation and security. We explain what happened and why it matters. We do not publish trading signals, price targets or personal investment recommendations.

Sourcing

Facts in our reporting come from sources we can point to: official statements, documents, on-chain data and credible news organisations. We link those sources and say plainly when something is not yet confirmed.

No endorsements

Coverage decisions are made editorially. A mention of a project, token, exchange or service is not an endorsement and not a recommendation to use or buy it.

Accuracy and corrections

Mistakes can happen in fast-moving news. When we find an error of fact, we correct the published article.

Information only

Nothing we publish is a recommendation to buy, sell or hold any asset. Crypto markets are volatile, and readers should make their own decisions and seek professional advice where needed.
