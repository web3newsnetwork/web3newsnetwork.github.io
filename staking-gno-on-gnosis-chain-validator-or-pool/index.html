Staking GNO on Gnosis Chain: validator or pool

Yes, you can stake GNO after bridging it to Gnosis Chain: deposit 1 GNO per validator or use a liquid pool; set a withdrawal address you control.

The Chain Times DeskNov 26, 2026 · 08:41 UTC2 min read

For an Ethereum GNO holder moving to Gnosis Chain, yes: bridge the tokens, then stake through a validator or a liquid staking pool.

Say you bridge 3 GNO from Ethereum. Gnosis Bridge moves it through Omnibridge, which locks tokens on Ethereum and mints them on Gnosis Chain, where GNO arrives at the same address. Use the bridge to gnosis chain option in Gnosis Bridge, and make sure you control that address on Gnosis Chain.

From there, the staking route depends on how much GNO you have and whether you want to run a node. Gnosis Chain staking happens through its deposit contract, where validator deposits enter the network.

Three GNO can fund three validators, or go into a liquid pool

Each Gnosis Chain validator requires a deposit of 1 GNO. In the 3 GNO case, you could fund three validators, depositing 1 GNO for each through the Gnosis Chain deposit contract.

With solo staking, you run a validator node and keep its rewards. That route suits someone willing to operate the node and able to commit GNO in 1 GNO increments. Validator rewards accrue in GNO on Gnosis Chain.

A liquid staking protocol can accept fractions of GNO and does not require you to run a node. In return, you receive a liquid staking token representing your stake. The protocol handles validator operation, but adds protocol risk and charges a fee.

For a validator deposit, choose the withdrawal credentials before depositing. These credentials determine where withdrawn GNO will go, and they are fixed at deposit time. Set them to an address you control on Gnosis Chain: withdrawn GNO lands at that address there. Check the address carefully before you submit the deposit, since you cannot change the credentials afterward.

When you want GNO on Ethereum again, first withdraw it to your Gnosis Chain address. Then use Gnosis Bridge to move it back across the chain. That return trip is a separate bridge transfer.

Related

Can I unstake GNO and bridge it back to Ethereum?

Yes. Withdraw GNO to the Gnosis Chain address in your withdrawal credentials, then bridge it from Gnosis Chain to Ethereum.

Do I need xDAI to stake GNO on Gnosis Chain?

The validator deposit is made in GNO. This staking route requires 1 GNO per validator; a liquid staking pool can accept fractions.
