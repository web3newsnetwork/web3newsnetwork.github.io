How to Swap Into a Protocol’s Minimum Deposit Token

A protocol’s minimum deposit token is the asset it accepts to open a position; check the network, amount and fees before swapping so the deposit arrives ready to use.

The Chain Times DeskOct 15, 2026 · 11:29 UTC3 min read

To meet a protocol’s minimum deposit, swap into the token and network its deposit page accepts, then send at least the stated amount. The token and minimum are different things: one names the asset, the other sets the smallest deposit. Check both in the protocol’s own instructions before you trade. For the bridge-and-swap steps, this guide to using ParaSwap to bridge and swap with fewer steps covers more detail.

How do I find the token and minimum?

Read the protocol’s deposit page or documentation, and confirm the accepted token, network and minimum amount there. A token with the same name can exist on several networks, but a deposit sent to the wrong network may not be credited. If the protocol lists more than one accepted asset, compare the minimum for each; they may differ.

Also check whether the displayed minimum is before or after any deposit fee. If the page does not make that clear, use the amount it shows as the minimum and leave a little extra room. A swap can return slightly less than its estimate when the price moves or the route incurs fees.

What should I check before swapping?

Start with the wallet and network you will use to deposit. The swap’s “from” token must be available in that wallet, and the “to” token must be the version accepted by the protocol on that network. A swap aggregator compares available trade routes and shows an estimated output; the transaction screen is where you check the actual quote before signing.

  • Confirm the destination token and network against the protocol’s deposit instructions.
  • Check the estimated amount received against the minimum, leaving room for price movement and fees.
  • Keep enough of the network’s native token to pay transaction fees, including any separate token approval the wallet requests.
  • Review the recipient and transaction details before signing.

How do I swap and make the deposit?

Choose the source token and amount, then select the required deposit token as the destination. Review the quote, including the estimated output and any price-impact or fee details shown. If the wallet asks you to approve the source token, check that request and sign it; then review and sign the swap transaction. A wallet approval gives a contract permission to use a token up to a stated limit, so check the amount shown.

After the swap confirms, open the protocol’s deposit page and connect the same wallet on the correct network. Enter an amount at or above the minimum, review the deposit transaction, and check the final amount received in the protocol. If the swap output is below the required minimum, swap more before depositing. The better choice for most readers is to leave a modest buffer above the minimum: it helps cover a shortfall without requiring an exact-output trade that may cost more or fail at the quoted rate.
